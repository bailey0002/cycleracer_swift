# KERB: GALACTIC — Game Design Document (current state, 2026-10-29)

**In plain English.** KERB: GALACTIC is a single-player iPhone game, played with a controller, set inside an arcade
cabinet: you ride a hoverboard (or a hover-speeder) through a neon city and a sunset canyon on timed courier jobs, and
settle scores with rivals in a Tron-style light-cycle arena. Three chapters and eighteen jobs carry a short story told
entirely in briefings, radio lines and messages: the "discs" you deliver are other riders' identities, and the last one
in the ledger is your own. Everything you can see is built and running in the prototype, including a tutorial layer of
rule cards, a per-chapter notebook and a goal card for each world; the first TestFlight build is archived and waits for
upload. What is still owed is mostly judgement: most of the content has been verified on the developer's capture tools
but not yet played and judged on a phone.

A living GDD: what the game *is today*, read from the code on `master` (PR #14, 18 Oct 2026; PRs #8–#14 merged since
the first edition, nothing has landed since 18 Oct). PR #7 `claude/garage` is still open and unjudged, noted where it
changes a number. Each element carries a status tag. It summarises; the sources of truth stay where they are:
`CLAUDE.md` (code map and gotchas), `SpeederProto/docs/polish-log.md` (what was built, with captures),
`SpeederProto/docs/assessment-2026-10-17-rd.md` (the R&D plan and its seven-step build order), `docs/game-direction.md`
(why a mission runner), `docs/assessment-2026-09-27-story.md` (the arc), `docs/arena-next.md` (the Grid's open
proposals), `SpeederProto/README.md` (harness, TestFlight). Supersedes `GDD_2026-10-17.md`. The interactive map is
`docs/gdd-map.html`.

**Status tags.** `BUILT` in the code · `CAPTURED` verified by the project's harness (Mac capture frames, simulator
sheets, the 19 headless tests) with evidence in `Captures/` or the log · `PHONE` judged on a phone with the Backbone
(the repo records the judgement) · `UNJUDGED` built but never judged on the phone · `DESIGNED` in a doc, not in code ·
`GAP` missing. No phone judgement has been recorded since 3 Oct 2026, so every `PHONE` tag below predates the
17 Oct edition.

## 4. Game flow (`Scene/FrontEnd.swift`, `Scene/GameController.swift`)

```
SPLASH (launch logo, Core Animation loader timed from the last launch; "Hideaway" starts here)
 → TITLE  (wordmark, camera drift; CONTINUE / START · CONTINUE SAVE · RIDERS · FREE PLAY · MESSAGES · SETTINGS)
   first run: → RIDERS (◀▶ rider, A) → STORY (10 beats, A hurry, B skip) → CALLSIGN + LIVERY (A = RIDE)
 → CHAPTER CARD (first job of a chapter, once; A) + the owner's interlude posted to the log
 → BRIEFING  (contact, brief, goal line, best / silver / gold, flags, GARAGE rows, INBOX chips, GOALS strip + chip,
             NOTES chip; at most one RULE CARD per look: goal card, garage, first side offer, notebook; A = ACCEPT)
   first job of a kind: → RULE CARD (RELAY / SWEEP / RUN / SALVAGE / DIVE; the world waits; A dismisses)
   duel: → RULE CARD (THE GRID, first time) → RIVAL INTRO card (3.6 s: name, temper, record, taunt; A skips)
 → RUN (HUD + comms lane + cue lane; Menu = PAUSE: RESUME · SAVE GAME · MESSAGES · NOTEBOOK · GOAL CARD · SETTINGS · QUIT)
   first respawn: RULE CARD (HULL RESTORED); first lost round: RULE CARD (DEREZZED)
 → RESULT card: success (title, payout, rank, flags, goal and notebook ticks, the debrief, the contact's line; A = NEXT JOB)
             or failed (reason, the contact's line; A = one-tap RETRY)
 → next BRIEFING (the inbox moved to the next open job)
FREE PLAY → WORLDS (Neon City / Sunset Canyon / The Grid) → run card → endless run or a first-to-three match → MATCH RESULT card → next rival
```

### 5.3 Rules that bite
- An idle pad does not own the steering: touch and keyboard still work with a pad connected.
- Boost disarms at 2 % energy and re-arms at 15 % (hysteresis), so a held trigger on an empty hull does not flicker.
- Cruise speed is locked while a job is live (the windows and the pursuer are tuned to 45 m/s).
- A hit takes 40 % of the speed, 70 ms of hit-stop, a sideways recoil (0.45 s) and `upgrades.hitDamage` of the bar.
- Pressing the lane limit (± 6.9 m) or the conduit wall scrapes: sparks, 0.08 bar / s.
- A rule card holds the world (`MissionRunner.update` waits) until it is dismissed.
- Haptics: the pad's own actuators when it has them, else the phone's (the Backbone has none, so the phone taptics
  do the work); a continuous engine hum from speed and boost; HAPTICS off stops everything.
- Demo mode pulses the accept button (0.5 s on, 1 s off) because a held accept has no edge.

## 6. Core mechanics

### 6.1 The corridor vehicle (`Scene/SpeederController.swift`, `Scene/WorldScroller.swift`) `BUILT PHONE`
The world scrolls past a vehicle fixed at z ≈ 0 in 40 m segments (eight live). Cruise 45 m/s (`FXSettings.cruiseSpeed`),
boost × 1.8 on the speeder (× `1 + 0.8 × rider boost` on the board), speed normalised against 110. Steering is a
velocity (`7.5 + 7 × speedNorm` m/s), altitude a second axis (hover 1.05 m to 5.6 m, climb 6.5 m/s, settles only below
1.6 m). Lane limit ± 6.9 m; the fork's divider is a lane limit past the nose (`wedgeLimit`). Section transitions are
blends (`tubeBlend`, `enclosure`, `forkBlend`): in a conduit the vehicle is clamped to a cylinder (radius 6 m − 0.7,
centre 6 m) and rolls toward the wall it hugs. Obstacles are pooled per segment in 1–3 rows across four lanes with two
lanes always open; blocks are jumped, drones ducked or shot, gates flown over. Plasma bolts: 95 m/s every 0.14 s
(`Scene/Weapons.swift`), a white core in a cyan sheath with Kenney's laser / blast clips. Hoverboard (the default):
the same controller with KERB's board, the rider posed by `Scene/RiderRig.swift` (no IK), 1.35 × scale, camera lift
0.9 / 1.9 m.

### 6.2 The job state machine (`Missions/MissionRunner.swift`) `BUILT CAPTURED PHONE`
`chapter → briefing → (rivalIntro) → running → success | failed`, plus `freePlay`; a rule card can hold any phase.
Per frame the runner takes distance, speed, hits, boost, scrape, kills, beacons and the branch taken, and feeds the
goal card, the notebook, the cue lane and the barks.

**One bar** (hull = boost fuel, 0…1, starts full):
| Drain / charge | Value |
|---|---|
| Hit | 25 % → 20 % → 16 % per HULL PLATING level, ÷ rider hull, × 2 on a dive; the HELMET takes the first hit |
| Scrape | 0.08 / s (× 2 on a dive) |
| Boost | 12 % → 9 % → 7 % per second per BOOST COIL level |
| Recharge | 0.035 / s when neither boosting nor scraping |
| Beacon | + 20 % · kill + 5 % |
| Empty bar | checkpoint respawn: hull to 50 %, streak reset, + 4 s on the clock; 2 per job (+ 1 with SPARE CORE); none left = HULL BREACHED |

**Streak score**: a gate every 200 m pays 50 × streak and refunds 1.5 s of the window; a beacon pays 150 × streak;
a kill 25 × streak without climbing it; the streak climbs to 8 and a hit resets it to 1. `maxScore` is the clean run
(gates and beacons climbing to the cap, beacons' extra 100 and kills at the cap); silver at 40 %, gold at 70 % of it.
Duels: gold on a win.

**Windows** (27 Sep 2026, from the headless loop): net cruise time at 45 m/s minus the gate refunds, plus 30 %, so
plain cruise arrives with about a quarter of the window left and the FAST flag (a third left) takes some boost.

**Escape**: the pursuer ramps to 47 m/s at 22 m/s²; gap += (speed − pursuer) × dt; CAUGHT at 4 m. Plain cruise is
caught by design. Heat (hits raise the pursuer's speed, a kiosk branch sheds it): `DESIGNED` (step 3).

**Flags**, once per job, kept forever: CLEAN (no hits), FAST (a third of the window left), GOLD (gold rank). They
open the side offers and tick notebook lines.

### 6.3 The light cycle (`Arena/LightCycle.swift`, `Arena/ArenaController.swift`, `Arena/TrailSystem.swift`) `BUILT PHONE`
Kinematic: the game owns speed, heading and position; lean, pitch and the damped visual heading are presentation.
Armagetron's accel curve: base 36 m/s, boost a burst at 34 m/s² up to 68, brake to 15, hard cap 96; decay toward
base at 0.30 from above (a grind's gain is kept a while) and 5.0 from below; every quarter turn multiplies speed
by 0.95; turn rate 2.1 rad/s, 25 % slower at full boost. Jump 12.5 m/s under 24 m/s² gravity, trail 2.2 m high.
The rival rides the same physics with a shorter burst (boost 54 at 24 m/s², cap 62: its 0.16 s decisions cannot
handle more; keep it that way). Feel tuning with the Backbone is still owed (§12).

| Arena rule | Value |
|---|---|
| Energy (boost meter) | starts 0.6; boost drains 0.22 / s, idle recharge 0.03 / s; a grind refills 0.35 × grind / s (0.12 while boosting); the rival has the same budget |

| Pickups | PHASE (A: pass through one wall within 5 s) · PULSE (A: erases the newest stretch of your own trail, a pulse runs back) · CHARGE (deck only, on contact: energy to full, + 20 m/s) |
